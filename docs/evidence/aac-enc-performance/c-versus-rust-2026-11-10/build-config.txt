## Timing harness and run order

- Both harnesses preload the same 16-bit PCM WAVs before timing.
- Both construct a fresh encoder per repetition and process the fixture from
  the beginning.
- Both feed exactly 1,024 sample frames per channel per call (the last call
  may be partial); this matches the C encoder's `frameLength` and the Rust
  `FRAME_LENGTH`.
- Both include the production output assembly/copy in the timed region but
  exclude output-buffer allocation, file writing, ADTS validation, and
  hashing.
- One untimed warm-up pass per process; 15 measured repetitions per workload
  in the native series; 10 in the portable series.
- Repetitions are repetition-major, workload-minor, matching the established
  checkpoint harness.
- Native series: 3 standalone C, 3 standalone Rust, 8 alternating C/R pairs,
  2 post C, 2 post Rust. Portable series: 8 alternating pairs.
- Alternating order: odd pairs C→R, even pairs R→C.

## Corpus

The 20-workload corpus is the established checkpoint corpus: the 19
`M58_FIXTURES` (15 deterministic synthetic plus four committed WAVs) and the
derived 40 s `m7-music-stereo-48k-x4` concatenation; 91.450 s of audio.
Canonical PCM was materialized from the published crate's own
`testdata::fixture_samples` generator; the four committed WAV PCM is read by
that same function. See `corpus-manifest.tsv` for every WAV SHA-256, PCM
FNV-1a, length, sample rate, channels, frames, and output hashes.
