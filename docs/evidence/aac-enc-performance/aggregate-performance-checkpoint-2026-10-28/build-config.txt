## Benchmark

```sh
AAC_PERF_BENCH=1 AAC_PERF_REPS=15 AAC_PERF_LONG=1 AAC_PERF_QUIET=1 \
  <binary> --ignored --nocapture \
  aac_enc::stream_driver::perf_tests::aac_perf_bench
```

- Harness: the repository's release test-build `aac_perf_bench` (`#[ignore]`,
  opt-in), production mode headline; feed/core also run by default.
- Corpus: the 19 committed `M58_FIXTURES` plus the derived
  `m7-music-stereo-48k-x4` 40 s concatenation; 91.45 s audio total.
- Inputs: 15 synthetic fixtures generated by committed test code; four
  committed WAVs:
  - `m7-music-stereo-48k.wav` `b49e10a1d3f51b80db1d4d1a1179afa9963570528042395df20be8e2193f3086`
  - `m7-speech-clean-stereo-48k.wav` `3dfb7dcb0cd9f600f9ae66eb791df6d7c740c833218df88f1de73f87353346ff`
  - `m7-speech-pauses-stereo-48k.wav` `67d69a64fed23fe16ab666a9e1f02262afa78934363c69999728cadd9ec1a4d3`
  - `m8-music-eroica-stereo-48k.wav` `4413c8a41fe148192081c504ecfb5d69b5c7c4fc158cefff8b0dfadf8923986b`
- Encoder: AAC-LC VBR-5, ADTS, 1024-sample frames, mono/stereo, 44.1/48 kHz,
  production `Encoder::new`/`push`/`flush`, 8,000 sample frames per channel.
- Warm-up: one complete untimed pass per process.
- Repetitions: 15 measured repetitions per fixture and mode; each repetition
  constructs a fresh encoder and processes the fixture from the beginning.
- Order: five standalone baseline runs (three before the paired series, two
  after) and eight paired old/new runs alternating old→new and new→old.
- Timing: `std::time::Instant` around `Encoder::new` (reported separately) and
  `push`/`flush`; fixture synthesis/decoding, interleaving, ADTS validation,
  hashing, and disk I/O are outside. Output bytes are copied into a
  preallocated buffer inside the timed region.
- Aggregation: the harness `AACPERF_AGG production` value is the median of
  complete-corpus per-repetition sums (all 20 workloads), not a sum of
  per-fixture medians. Pair improvement is
  `(old_agg - new_agg) / old_agg × 100`, positive = current faster.
- Hash gate: the harness asserts every fixture's committed byte total and
  FNV-1a hash on macOS aarch64; `REPS=1` runs produced the 60-row verification
  logs used for the explicit baseline/current comparison.

The exact test-only files used to build the measured harness (`stream_driver.rs`,
`production_baseline.rs`, the `aac_enc.rs` registration, and the production
example) are preserved under `harness/` with instructions. They are not
production source changes and are not part of the optimization commit.
