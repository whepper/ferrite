- Bands 38,39 carry NONZERO spectra on both sides (group2 band10/11 lines [716,752)/[752,800): live mid ±14–28k, real retained-L ±8–26k; live ch1 side zeroed, real ch1 retained). - The bulk mechanism is ch0-wide per-line spectral residue BEYOND the stored-energy envelope: live-vs-real post-MS spectra show 372 active lines out-of-envelope on ch0 (e.g. idx413: −191300 vs −191904, tol 415; idx423: 343768 vs 344958, tol 721) while band energies stay 100% in-envelope and ch1-side energies are bit-exact. These line-level deltas feed S4 (`EstimateScaleFactors`/`QuantizeSpectrum` consume spectra, not just energies), shifting scf/Q/section books — the ≈+206 dyn remainder.
- Static +18 cannot come from the mask flip: MS side-info bit count depends only on digest+counts (`bitenc.cpp:412-421`: 2 + 52 bits for SOME), never on mask values. Prime suspect is TNS side-info (orders/lengths/coefs live-vs-real unobserved — no live-TNS hook exists; V4.24 verified only orders vs the oracle, never live-vs-real TNS). Recorded as bounded-unresolved.
- A third, unexcluded contributor is QC-grant history: live `gains=[-47,0]` vs unknown real gains; grants evolve from prior-frame bitres, and live-vs-real LONG-history PE was never compared frame-by-frame. Recorded as hypothesized, not evidenced.
- Verdict for the +28 B: narrowed to (residue→38,39 mask flip [tens of bits]) + (ch0-wide TNS-lane spectral residue → S4/Q/section shifts [bulk]) + (static +18, TNS side-info suspect). Full bit-attribution requires real S4 scf/gain/Q + S5 sections/dyn + S6 grants and live TNS side-info — none available. Cause of the full gap NOT YET PROVEN.
