## 3. Owner decisions — P findings

### 3.1 F-07, F-09, F-10, F-15, F-17 and F-18

**Disposition: CONDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE**

These six findings arise from the I2 sentinel-escape mechanism. A one-stored-LSB difference changes the I2 boundary result and allows the FPE arm to enter an IS coding path where the Q31 arm remains MS-coded.

The panned and out-of-phase probe sequences show persistence over the observed five-frame interval. The out-of-phase cases use the out-of-phase IS path.

**Owner rationale and condition:**

> The observed five-frame persistence is understood, but termination is not proven.

The conditional acceptance is limited to the observed behaviour and the evidence currently available. It does not claim that the cascade terminates on longer material or that the resulting signal differences are perceptually neutral.

No general termination proof is required to record this owner disposition, but the limitation must remain visible in the milestone record.

## 4. Owner decisions — G findings

### 4.1 Scale-only differences

**Findings:** F-19, F-21, F-23, F-24 and F-25
**Disposition: ACCEPT**

These findings change the IS scalefactor while retaining the same IS book and phase on both arms.

The numerical differences are explained by the captured energy and scalefactor calculations. The relevant probe material was subsequently covered by the G-1 energy-envelope validation.

F-24 is the largest energy difference in this group and is not a sub-LSB difference. It remains accepted based on the trace evidence and the applicable envelope validation, not on an assumption that the numerical difference is negligible.

**Owner rationale:**

> Accept the five localized scalefactor differences. Same IS book and phase; numerical differences are explained and energy-envelope validated.

Acceptance does not imply bitstream equivalence or perceptual neutrality.

### 4.2 IS admission / direction-decision differences

**Findings:** F-20, F-22 and F-26
**Disposition: CONDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE**

These findings arise from a one-stored-LSB difference in right-channel energy. The Q31 and FPE paths consequently obtain different Ld values and different `real_scale` outcomes. The Q31 path drops the band, while the FPE path retains it for IS coding.

The MS first-pass decision agrees between the two arms. The divergence is in IS processing.

The numerical mechanism and resulting coding behaviour are understood and trace-verified. The underlying energy differences satisfy the approved energy-envelope validation. However, per-finding PE/ATS evidence is unavailable, and no perceptual impact assessment has been established.

**Owner rationale and condition:**

> The one-LSB energy distinction and resulting IS direction decision are understood and trace-verified. The energy envelope is satisfied, but no per-finding PE/ATS or perceptual evidence is available. Conditional acceptance, with the explicit limitation that the numerical and coding behaviour is understood, but per-finding downstream/perceptual impact has not been demonstrated.

This is an explicit acceptance of the observed numerical and coding behaviour subject to the stated evidence limitation. It is not a finding of perceptual harmlessness.
