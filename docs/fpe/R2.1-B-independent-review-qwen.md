  owner acceptance (§10). No new tolerance is needed or permitted.

## 2. Evidence classes used in this review

Every claim below is tagged with one of:

- **RUN** — independently reproduced by executing retained repository tests
  (commands and outputs in §3.1).
- **SRC** — verified by reading production/test source (file:line cited).
- **REP** — independently recomputed arithmetic from owner-reported operands
  using a scratch program outside the repo (§3.3). This verifies internal
  consistency of the reported numbers, **not** the operands themselves.
- **OWN** — owner-reported only; produced by the temporary dump harness that
  was removed after the owner review. Not reproducible from retained
  artifacts. The retained tests record finding *existence/counts* but do not
  print per-finding operands, margins, or trace chains.

The distinction between REP and RUN matters: the owner's per-finding values
(`e`, `eld`, `tld`, `corr`, `real`, `M`, `B`, event chains) come from a
**removed** temporary harness (`R21B-DUMP` appears nowhere in retained code —
verified by grep). I could verify that the reported numbers are mutually
consistent and consistent with production semantics to the unit, but a future
reader cannot re-derive them from the delivered tests alone. This is an
evidence-retention limitation (R-6), not a correctness defect: the owner
disclosed the method, and the retained gates re-validate the structural
claims on every run.

## 7. Status semantics: `OutOfEnvelope` ≠ numerical-bound violation; `B_closed` applies to MS only

This distinction is load-bearing and the owner review mostly respects it; I
state it explicitly and flag one slip.

- **`ValStatus::OutOfEnvelope` on IS findings** (`is_ms.rs:2069-2073`) means
  "no local IS decision bound is claimed here" — it is a routing label for
  coding-state divergences outside the validated-bound domain. It does
  **not** assert that the underlying energy difference violates the R1
  `tol_E(b)` envelope, and it is not a `BoundFailure`. The harness has no
  numerical bound for IS admission at all (I1–I10 are exact decision
  *explanations*, not tolerances). Conflating the label with an envelope
  violation would be a category error; the owner does not make it.
- **`B_closed`** (`is_ms.rs:309-314`) bounds the **MS first-pass margin
  shift** `|M_q − M_f|` in its supported domain (finite Ld operands, non-IS
  on both arms). A violation is `BoundFailure` → panic → investigation; none
  occurred (RUN). IS findings are never `B_closed` violations. The owner's
  §2 closing claim ("no P/G finding is a decision change outside a derived
  bound; MS-side … agrees everywhere in-domain") is accurate.
- **Slip (R-5):** F-19's "shift 369256 ≤ B=369260 — **inside the
  supported-domain bound**" (owner lines 274-275) and the G-context "two
  bands with shift inside `B_closed`" mislabel the domain. b23/b37 are
  IS-coded on **both** arms (book 15/15), so `ms_band_supported` returns
  false and the harness performs **no** `B_closed` validation there; the
  quoted margins/bounds are would-be first-pass values from the dump
  (arithmetically correct — I reproduced B=369260 exactly — but
  supplementary, not harness-validated). The same applies to F-20/F-22/F-26's
  "MS identical, B=3" notes (FPE IS bands). The substantive fact — MS
  first-pass **use** agrees on all eight bands — stands; the domain label
  must be corrected to "IS-excluded; would-be margin arithmetic satisfied".

## 10. Critical question: R1 energy-envelope coverage of the probe corpus

### 10.1 Decision

**Yes — this is an acceptance gap. R1 envelope validation of the R2.1-B probe
corpus (using the existing approved envelope, unchanged) is REQUIRED before
owner acceptance of F-03…F-30.** F-01/F-02 are exempt: their fixture

owner's §4 envelope-standing claim for that frame is verified.

### 10.2 Why it is a gap (not a scope nicety)

1. **The harness itself assigns the responsibility to R1 and R1 has not
   discharged it.** Gate 6 compares geometry only; its comment states stored
   value vectors are "NOT compared here — numerical equivalence is owned by
   the R1 energy envelope and the margin analysis below"
   (`is_ms.rs:1366-1368`). The R1 full-corpus audit list
   (`differential.rs:201-227`) contains **none** of the six probes (nor
   silence-as-probe; the silence *fixture* is present). The probes exist only
   in `is_ms.rs`. So the numerical equivalence of the probe operands is
   delegated but unperformed.
2. **Cause B is the acceptance route, and it is unanchored for the probes.**
   `route()` accepts Cause B findings with documented evidence
   (`is_ms.rs:216-227`). "B = numerical energy/psychoacoustic difference"
   means *expected representation residue* as opposed to implementation
   defect (C). The approved discriminator between residue and defect is the
   derived envelope (`05` §9.2/§9.6; `07` §5.1 "FPE adopts, does not widen").
   Margin/trace evidence explains **how** an operand difference propagates
   into a decision; it cannot establish **that the operand difference itself
   is legitimate**. A 1-LSB delta at an `fmult` boundary is consistent with
   both expected residue and a subtle FPE energy-lane rounding bug. For
   F-01/F-02 the R1 gate anchors B; for F-03…F-30 nothing does.
3. **The project's own acceptance criterion requires it.** `07` §5.2:
   "FPE passes a stage iff (a) every pure-integer/decision field matches
   exactly (or is a classified pinned exception), and (b) every value field
   is within its envelope. A single unexplained out-of-envelope value or new
   decision flip fails the stage." The 28 probe flips are "new decision flips
   requiring triage (residue-explained vs structural)" (`07`:235-237); triage
   is incomplete until the residue is envelope-adjudicated. `05` §9.6 makes
   the split explicit: envelope = energy-value equivalence; R2 = decision
   safety; **both** are required before FPE-3.1 acceptance.
4. **The owner review contradicts itself here.** §4 admits the probe deltas
   "are not separately envelope-adjudicated … envelope adjudication belongs
   to R1", while §6 claims "Cannot yet decide: **nothing on the evidence
   axis** — every finding is individually grounded above." Both cannot hold:
   the missing adjudication is evidence-axis grounding for the Cause-B
   classification. The §6 claim is an unsupported conclusion (R-1).

### 10.3 Can the existing envelope be applied? Yes — per-material analysis

The machinery is generic: `EnergyAudit::compare_channel` takes a
`ChannelEnergyCtx` (pre-rescale f64 spectrum, grouping, active bands,
`SfbConfig`, TNS activity) plus the two `ChannelOutput`s
(`audit.rs:312-321,519-1005`). The probes are deterministic PCM
(`probe_sine`/`probe_narrow`, `is_ms.rs:1741-1783`) and the production
frontend feeds them through the **same** `process_pair_reuse` /
`fpe::process_pair_reuse` engines the R1 `DiffStream` drives
(`stream_driver.rs:957-984` vs `tests/mod.rs:482-525`). So a
`DiffStream::run_checked`-style scenario per probe is directly constructible
with **zero envelope changes**. Semantic bridge to the decision operands:
short-frame IS/MS `e_ld = ld_of(grouped stored)` and thresholds
`threshold_ld_of(t_stored)` are **exact integer functions** of the audited
stored vectors on both arms (`stream_driver.rs:146-165,1081-1094`); the long
MS Ld lane's `q31_of_ld` rounding (≤1 i32 LSB) corresponds to a relative
energy factor ≈2.1e-8, negligible against the envelope's `E_ref/500` term
(2e-3). Envelope conformance of the stored/float lanes therefore covers the
decision operands, with the conversion exactness already asserted by R1
(MsOutput/grouped-MS bit-exact, `mod.rs:158-163,234-239`) and R2.1-B replays.

- **48 kHz panned & out-of-phase probes (LONG + START/SHORT/STOP at onset
  f4):** squarely within the envelope's designed scope (long/short rescale,
  spread propagation, grouped sums, TNS-reuse `q31_tol`). The I2-escape root
  is a stored-energy Δ of **1**, below the envelope's floor of 32 wherever
  the band is supported — conformance is *expected to be trivial*, but the
  supported-domain status of these very quiet bands (shift-clamp /
  accumulation / saturation guards, `envelope.rs:159-176,218-275`) is itself
  unverified and must be reported, not assumed. The eld deltas feeding
  `real_scale` need the energy-domain inversion check (`audit.rs:32-38,
  479-514`).
- **44.1 kHz panned probe:** rate-generic via `SfbConfig`; R1 already
  validates both 44100 fixtures (RUN #2). Same expectations; the flip bands
  differ (b5/b6/b15) because SFB widths differ — per-band adjudication is
  exactly what the envelope provides.
- **Short-window narrow-band probe (F-19…F-26):** grouped energies and
  grouped Ld are audited with summed per-window bounds
  (`audit.rs:1039-1053,1119-1128`); any unsupported window invalidates the
  grouped bound (`:1042-1044`). The 0-vs-1 stored transitions are §9.4
  **Class B** by construction and the scale-edge eld deltas are low-energy
  rounding phenomena — the audit's sentinel classification
